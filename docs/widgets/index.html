NotionMake widgets

Small, free widgets you can embed in Notion: paste a link into an /embed block. They run entirely in your browser, with no tracking, cookies or network calls. NotionMake's AI adds them for you; you can also build a link yourself.

  • clock.html – tz, style=digital|analog, hour12=1, seconds=0, date=0, label
  • timer.html – mode=pomodoro|countdown|stopwatch, focus, break, long, rounds, minutes, auto=1
  • countdown.html – date=YYYY-MM-DD, time=HH:MM, label
  • calendar.html – start=mon|sun, mark=YYYY-MM-DD,..., month=YYYY-MM
  • checklist.html – tasks you can tick with a progress bar that follows them: items=Task one|Task two (up to 30), title, done=1,3 (pre-ticked)
  • progress.html – type=day|week|month|year|custom, start, end, label
  • quote.html – tag=any|study|focus|calm

All widgets accept theme=auto|dark|terracotta|sage|periwinkle|navy|teal|rose.
